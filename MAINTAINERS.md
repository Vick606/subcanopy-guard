## What maintainers do

- Review and merge pull requests
- Triage issues and discussions
- Cut releases and publish to PyPI
- Respond to security disclosures (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md))

## Becoming a maintainer

There is no formal process yet. If you have contributed consistently and
would like to help maintain the project, open a discussion.
